=========================================================
IMAGE CREDITS & HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN PHOTOS LATER
=========================================================

1. CURRENT DEMO ARTWORK:
   Right now, all event and gallery visuals in this demo are
   custom inline SVG illustrations generated locally inside
   script.js. That means zero external links can break and
   the site loads super fast on mid-range phones!

2. HOW TO ADD YOUR REAL COLLEGE PHOTOS LATER:
   Step A: Compress your photos first (keep them under 200-300 KB
           using a free compressor or phone resize) so the page
           stays fast on mobile data.
   Step B: In GitHub, open the "assets/images/" folder and click
           "Add file" -> "Upload files" to upload your photos
           (for example: concert-night.jpg, food-stall.jpg).
   Step C: Open "data.js", find the event or gallery item, and
           change:
               image: "",
           to:
               image: "assets/images/concert-night.jpg",

3. IF YOU USE FREE STOCK PHOTOS (UNSPLASH / PEXELS):
   Write the photographer's name and photo link right here in
   this file to keep proper credits!
